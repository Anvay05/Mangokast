09

Product principles

The rules that make Mangokast worth trusting.

These principles are the product moat—not a shiny score or a crowded dashboard.

Outcome over vanity

Followers are context. Budget-fit reach, clicks, purchases, and margin are decisions.

Explain every score

A marketer should know why a creator is recommended and what is still unknown.

Test before scaling

Use small controlled bets to learn before sending the whole budget to one creator.

Facts ≠ estimates

Separate public API facts, creator-authorized data, Mangokast assumptions, and actual outcomes.

Build the system that learns which creator is worth the next rupee.
