YouTube creator intelligence for measurable growth

Find creators who make the business case work.

Mangokast helps a business move from a campaign goal to a clear creator shortlist, then from a shortlist to tracked purchases and real CAC.

A decision system, not just a creator directory.
01

The product in one sentence

Give us the brief. We find, explain, test, and learn.

Mangokast is designed for brands that want to spend their creator budget carefully. It uses public YouTube signals, creator-authorized information, campaign economics, and actual outcomes.

01 / ONBOARD

Set up the brand

Save the category, product price, margin, target audience, market, language, and brand safety preferences.

02 / BRIEF

Define success

Set the campaign goal, budget, target CAC, conversion assumptions, dates, and deliverables.

03 / RESEARCH

Find and compare

Discover creators or import channel links. Review recent performance, fit, fee, risks, and missing data.

04 / LEARN

Track the result

Use unique links and codes to compare expected versus actual clicks, purchases, revenue, and CAC.

Cost to serve one business

What does one creator search really cost?

The current local MVP does not cost $47 for one influencer. The direct software cost is close to zero because the app runs on the local machine and uses the official YouTube API quota.

Important correction

$47 is not the cost of one influencer.

It was a rough monthly allocation for shared hosting, optional AI, and outreach. It should never be presented as the cost of one creator analysis.
